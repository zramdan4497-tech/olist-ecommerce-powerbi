Excited to share my final project for the Data Analysis Diploma: a full Power BI analysis of 100,000+ real e-commerce orders from Olist, Brazil's largest marketplace.

What I built:
- Cleaned and modeled 9 related tables (112K+ order lines) with Power Query into a star schema
- 24 DAX measures covering sales, customers, payments, delivery and reviews
- A 5-page interactive dashboard: Overview, Sales & Products, Customers & Geography, Delivery Performance, and Reviews

What the data says:
- R$ 13.6M in product revenue across 99,441 orders (2016-2018)
- 91.9% of orders arrive before the promised date - and late deliveries cut the average review score from 4.3 to 2.6
- Only 3.1% of customers ever came back for a second purchase
